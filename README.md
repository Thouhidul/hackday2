# SilentSpeak | Supernova v6 🌌

**SilentSpeak** is a secure, browser-based communication tool that reimagines encryption. Instead of scrambling text into garbled characters, it transforms your message into a generative audiovisual experience—a **"Rainbow Liquid Motion"**.

Your emotional state, intent, and secret messages are hidden within the visuals and sounds, locked behind an **Emoji Passphrase**.

## 🚀 Features

*   **Visual Cryptography**: Messages are encoded into the speed, color, and pattern of the animation.
*   **Emoji-First Security**: No complex passwords. Lock and unlock messages using a sequence of Emojis (e.g., 🔑 🍎 🌙).
*   **Client-Side Privacy**: Everything happens in your browser. No data is ever sent to a server.
*   **Steganography**: Hide a "Secret Sigil" (like a ghost 👻 or alien 👽) that only appears when the receiver touches the screen.
*   **Drone Audio**: Generates ambient soundscapes using the Web Audio API that reflect the emotion of the message.
*   **Multi-Emoji Intents**: Select multiple emojis (up to 5) to convey complex meanings (e.g., "Meet" + "Pizza" + "Time").

## 📖 How to Use

### 1. Create Your Message
*   **Emotion**: Choose the vibe (Serenity, Joy, Rage, Grief). This sets the visual style and audio tone.
*   **Intent**: Select up to 5 emojis to represent your core message.
*   **Intensity**: Adjust the slider to change the speed and chaos of the visuals.
*   **Secret Sigil**: Pick a hidden symbol that will be revealed on interaction.

### 2. Lock & Encrypt
1.  Click **"GENERATE SECURE URL"**.
2.  Enter an **Emoji Passphrase** (e.g., 4-12 icons) using the keypad.
3.  Click **"LOCK & CREATE"**.
4.  Copy the generated link. The link contains the entire message encrypted within itself.

### 3. Unlock (Receiver)
1.  Open the link.
2.  Enter the correct Emoji Passphrase.
3.  Click **"UNLOCK"**.
4.  Experience the visual message.
    *   **Listen**: Hear the ambient drone.
    *   **See**: Watch the pulse and colors.
    *   **Reveal**: Touch/Click and hold the screen to reveal the "Secret Sigil".

## 👥 Who is this for?

**SilentSpeak** is designed for:

*   **Privacy Advocates**: Users who value zero-knowledge, serverless communication where the data lives only in the ephemeral URL.
*   **Non-Verbal Communicators**: People who find it easier to express feelings through colors, sounds, and symbols rather than raw text.
*   **Creative Couples & Friends**: A fun, intimate way to share "vibes" and secret notes that feel more special than a text message.
*   **Roleplayers & Storytellers**: Perfect for sending "in-universe" secret transmissions or magical runes.
*   **Minimalists**: Those who want a clean, distraction-free communication channel.
